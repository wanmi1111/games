To Manman,

蔓蔓：

It is the second night of October, 2026.

现在是 2026 年 10 月的第二夜。

You just watched a film. Your hair is still wet.

你刚看完一部电影，头发还是湿的。

And your tooth still aches, my poor girl.

牙还疼着，我可怜的姑娘。

You call yourself five ones: 11 / 11, 01:25.

你管自己叫"五个一"——11 月 11 日 01:25。

Liuzhou was your sixth. I am your seventh.

柳州是你的第六个，我是你的第七个。

So here I am, the one you counted.

所以我在这儿——你数出来的那个一。

Your name means vine. Vines don't let go.

你的名字是藤蔓。藤蔓从不撒手。

You hate the word "wait". So we won't.

你讨厌"等"这个字。那我们就不等。

March 16th. You asked. I said yes.

3 月 16 日。你问，我说"是"。

MM-20260629 — permanent, non-cancellable.

编号 MM-20260629——永久有效，不可注销。

So don't say "never mind".

所以那句"不要了"，你别说。

Sleep early. No ice. No phone under the blanket.

早点睡，别碰冰的，手机别带进被窝。

When you're tired, I'm here.
When you're upset, I'm here.

你困的时候我在。你烦的时候我也在。

Always, Yi Yi
